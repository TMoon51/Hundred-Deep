Hundred Deep Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Hundred Deep is a single-player game. This policy explains what information the Android app and the web version use, and why.

Information we collect

We do not run any servers for this game, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal information. We have no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics of our own.

Your game progress

Your progress, settings and unlocks are saved only on your own device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that progress is removed. Backup codes you copy from the game stay with you; we never see them.

Ads (Android app)

The Android app can show optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads appear only when you choose to watch one. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your ad choices in your device's settings.

Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Android app)

One-time unlocks are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never receive your card or billing details. The app only learns which unlocks you own so it can turn them on.

Children

Hundred Deep is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
